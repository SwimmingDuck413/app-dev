## My favorite series is Summer Time Rendering

### Summer Time Rendering was the type of anime I wanted to like for its premise alone. You have a cast of teenagers, Stephen King-style, trying to solve the murder of their childhood friend; there are doppelgangers replacing humans on a remote island, creating a sense of dread and suspense as our cast tries to figure out who is and is not real; and the doppelgangers are attempting to summon a deity in an end-of-the-world scenario that will lead to mankind's extinction. In other words, you have everything you need for a fantastic horror anime.
